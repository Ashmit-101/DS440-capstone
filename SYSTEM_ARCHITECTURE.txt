FEATURE MAPPING (What Makes This Work)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

WITHOUT Health Data (Old Way):
┌─────────────┐
│sleep_quality│ (1-5 scale)  ──mapsto──> sleep_hours: 4.5-8.75 hrs
└─────────────┘                         [Arbitrary mapping]

WITH Health Data (New Way):
┌─────────────┐
│sleep_quality│ (1-5 scale)  ──ignored──> 
└─────────────┘

┌───────────────┐
│sleep_hours    │ (from CSV)  ──mapsto──> 7.5 hrs [REAL DATA]
└───────────────┘


WITHOUT Health Data (Old Way):
┌───────────┐
│energy_     │ (1-5 scale)  ──average with calmness──> activity_mean: ~2.5
└───────────┘                [Proxy guess]

WITH Health Data (New Way):
┌────────┐
│ steps  │ (from CSV)  ──formula──> activity_mean: min(5, max(1, steps/4000))
└────────┘                        8200 steps → 2.05 [REAL DATA]


WITHOUT Health Data (Old Way):
┌──────────┐
│exercise_ │ (depends on survey)  ──average──> exercise_intensity: ~2-3
└──────────┘                       [Proxy guess]

WITH Health Data (New Way):
┌──────────────┐
│calories_burn │ (from CSV)  ──formula──> exercise: (cal - 1000) / 400
└──────────────┘                       2150 cal → 2.875 [REAL DATA]
